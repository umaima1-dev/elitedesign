The honest bit

Clip It isn’t a one-click web app, and I’d rather you know that now. It finds moments from what people say, so it works best on videos with speech.

Setup

You need Claude Code installed and logged in. Setup is one script that installs anything else that’s missing and checks everything works. You run it once.

Speed

Speed depends on your computer. A one-hour video transcribes in roughly 2 to 8 minutes on a newer Mac or a PC with an NVIDIA graphics card. On an ordinary laptop processor it’s more like 15 to 40 minutes. Rendering adds a bit on top.

Rights

Use it on content you own or have permission to repurpose. Your own videos, your client’s videos with their OK, a guest’s footage with theirs.
