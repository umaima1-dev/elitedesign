What Clip It does

Press the button. Here’s what happens.

  1. 1

    Step 1

    It finds the moments worth posting.

    Claude reads the whole transcript, start to finish, and picks the clips with a real hook. You can steer it:

    • “5 clips, focus on the money advice”
    • “Only the bits about relationships”
  2. 2

    Step 2

    It keeps the right person in frame.

    Face tracking and active-speaker detection follow whoever is talking and crop to 9:16 automatically. Two people on one camera is fine.

  3. 3

    Step 3

    It captions every word, on time.

    Transcription runs on your computer with Whisper, timed to the word. Pick from four caption styles:

    PopBig pop words
    BoxTikTok-style word boxes
    KaraokeKaraoke fill
    Clean subtitlesClean subtitles
    Clip 10
  4. 4

    Step 4

    It adds the edit you’d normally pay an editor for.

    Text is placed away from the speaker’s face, so nothing covers anyone.

    • Punch zooms
    • Shake
    • Pop-up callouts
    • Counting stat cards
    • Name tags
    • Emoji bursts
  5. 5

    Step 5

    It writes the hook for each one.

    Every reel gets a hook title on screen, plus a ready-to-paste post caption and hashtags in a text file next to the video.

    • Hook title on screen
    • Post caption
    • Hashtags
  6. 6

    Step 6

    It makes the cover too.

    Each reel comes with a cover image, so you’re not hunting for a frame where nobody is mid-blink.
