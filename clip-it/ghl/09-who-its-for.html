Who it’s for

Clip It is for you if you record long-form video every week and the clips keep not happening.

  • Podcasters
  • YouTubers
  • Course creators
  • Coaches and speakers
  • Social media managers
  • Editors

Agencies get the most out of it.

Ten clients’ videos would cost you ten seats or a top-tier plan elsewhere. Here it’s the same tool on the same computer, with each client’s branding saved in their own settings.

It’s probably not for you if you never want to install anything. That’s fine. The hosted tools exist for exactly that.
