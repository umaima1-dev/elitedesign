You recorded a great video.

Ninety minutes of good footage, and somewhere in there are six or seven moments that would stop a scroll.

Finding them is the job nobody wants. You scrub through the timeline, write down timestamps, cut, reframe so your speaker isn’t half off the screen, fix captions word by word, and export. One video eats an afternoon. So most weeks the video goes up and the clips never happen.

The tools that fix this charge you every month for the privilege. They’re good tools. They also meter you in credits, and the bill keeps coming whether you posted ten reels that month or none.
