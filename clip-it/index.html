Why it runs on your computer

This is the part that makes Clip It different from the subscription tools.

Downloading, transcription, cutting, face tracking and rendering all happen on your own machine. Claude is only called once per video, to choose the clips and plan the edit, and the dashboard shows you exactly how many tokens that used. You see the number. Nothing is hidden behind a credit system.

Your videos stay on your computer. Nothing gets uploaded to someone else’s platform to be processed.

And because it’s yours, you can change anything: fonts, colours, caption style, your handle on every reel, the end card, background music. Set it once and every reel follows it. Want a different look for one episode? Change just that episode.

The honest bit

Clip It isn’t a one-click web app, and I’d rather you know that now.

Setup

You need Claude Code installed and logged in. Setup is one script that installs anything else that’s missing and checks everything works. You run it once.

Speed

Speed depends on your computer. A one-hour podcast transcribes in roughly 2 to 8 minutes on a newer Mac or a PC with an NVIDIA graphics card. On an ordinary laptop processor it’s more like 15 to 40 minutes. Rendering adds a bit on top.

Rights

Use it on content you own or have permission to repurpose. Your podcast, your client’s podcast with their OK, your guest’s episode with theirs.

Questions, answered

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. No subscription, no credits, no paid add-ons, and no other app to sign up for. The only thing it runs on is the Claude Code you already use, and each video uses a small amount of your Claude usage to pick the clips. The dashboard shows you exactly how much for every run.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Run one setup script, then use the browser dashboard: paste a link, press a button. If you’re comfortable in Claude Code you can drive it with commands instead.

Mac or Windows?

Both, plus Linux. There’s a setup script for each.

Can it handle two people on one camera?

Yes. Active-speaker detection follows whoever’s mouth is moving. For tricky shots you can switch a clip to a blurred-background layout instead.

Can I use my own fonts, colours and handle?

Yes. Seven fonts are bundled, highlight colours are yours to set, and your handle and end card go on every reel.

Does it post for me?

No. You get the finished videos, captions and covers in a folder. You post them, or drop them into your scheduler.

Can I use it on someone else’s podcast?

Only with their permission. Use it on content you have the rights to.
