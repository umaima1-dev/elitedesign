What you build, day by day.

Every lesson follows the same rhythm. You learn the idea, watch me build it, then build your own version using your real work.

Section One

Foundations

Days 01 to 02
01

Claude Fundamentals

Where everything lives, which model to use for which job, and why long chats go off the rails. Then the part people don't expect: Claude using your computer for you. You finish with your setup done and a time audit of your week, which becomes the number you measure everything against.

02

Your Context System

You build an organised folder system on your own computer, so Claude knows who you are, how you work and what every project is about. This is the day you stop explaining yourself from scratch.

Section Two

Claude In Real Work

Days 03 to 07
03

Writing In Your Voice

04

Claude For Design

Your style becomes a design system Skill. You build a carousel, a page and a slide deck that all look like they belong together.

05

Content

Hooks, scripts and captions, plus how one idea becomes a week of posts.

06

Your Tools, Data And Documents

Claude connected to your inbox, calendar and Drive. The 40-page PDF you've been avoiding gets summarised, compared with last year's version and turned into a chart.

07

Growth

Finding the right people, researching them and writing messages that mention what they actually do. You can use it for clients, job applications, networking or research.

Section Three

Claude Code And AI Agents

Days 08 to 11
08

Claude Code Foundations

It looks intimidating for about ten minutes. You learn the two habits that make it easy. Claude interviews you before it starts anything, and it shows you a plan before it touches a single file.

09

Anatomy Of An AI Agent

Every agent has the same seven parts. Once you can see them, agents stop feeling like magic and become something you can design.

10

Build Your First Agent

You build a real agent for a real task from your own week. It only gets the access it needs, it asks before anything goes out, and it runs on a schedule while you sleep.

11

Creative Work With Claude Code

You edit a short video with captions and motion graphics in your own colours, without opening editing software.

Section Four
