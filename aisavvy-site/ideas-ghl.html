Your ideas were never the problem.

The problem is that every idea needs your hands on it. You can only write so much, design so much and research so much before the day runs out. So the urgent things get done and the interesting ideas wait for a quiet week that never comes.

AI fluent people work differently. They have an idea, hand it to Claude with the right context and get back a first version to shape. Then they're onto the next one.

This is happening in every field right now. The student finishes the research in an evening, the employee's report is ready before the meeting, and the creator posts every day without burning out. They aren't smarter than you, and most of them aren't technical. They just learned to operate the tool everyone else is still chatting with.
