Job descriptions are changing faster than most people notice.

"Experience with AI tools" used to be a nice extra at the bottom of a job post. Now it's moving into the requirements.

You can be excellent at what you do. You can have the degree, the experience and the reputation. In 2027, the most valuable person in the room will be the one who's great at their work and knows how to hand part of it to AI.

That's the priority now. You don't need to work harder. You need to know how to make AI do some of the work with you.

When Microsoft and LinkedIn surveyed 31,000 people across 31 countries
