Test photos for SketchForge (all public domain / CC0, from scikit-image's sample data).

1-portrait-astronaut   face + fabric + hard edges. Try Pencil ("Realistic pencil portrait"), then draw a
                       detail box over the face. Paint by numbers: "Portrait (28 colours)".
2-pet-cat              fur texture, soft focus. Pencil ("Soft pencil"); Paint by numbers 12-24 colours.
3-still-life-coffee    clear shapes and shadows. Outline ("Clean outline to trace"), Hatch, and Tones.
4-landscape-rocket     big simple regions. Paint by numbers "Beginner (6 colours)" shows the cleanest result.
5-hard-case-...        dark and blurry on purpose: shows what happens with a poor customer photo.

Real customer photos are the real test: use sharp, well-lit ones, 1500px or larger.
